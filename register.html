?How do I confirm my email?
  1. Type your own email address carefully, then select Send confirmation link.
  2. Open the most recent confirmation email. If it does not arrive, check spam and wait before requesting another one.
  3. Follow the link back to this workshop. Enter the same email address and select Confirm registration.
  4. Wait for Email confirmed, then continue. At your next start, enter your email again. A remembered, valid registration lets you continue without another email.

If the link expired, request a new one. On a shared computer, leave Remember unchecked and use Forget this browser when finished.

!What is recorded?

When registration opens, your email address, confirmation date and workshop registration dates are stored privately for EFI BITCom workshop administration. Supabase Auth and the configured email service process the confirmation email. Your address is not published on GitHub.

Remembering this browser saves an opaque workshop-only token, not your password or a Supabase login. Your email is requested again at each start. Your workbook answers stay in your browser unless you download or share them yourself; registration does not synchronise your answers between devices.

Forgetting the browser removes its remembered token; it does not erase the organiser’s registration record. Ask the workshop facilitator about access, retention or deletion.
