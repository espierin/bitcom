?How does the email link work?
  1. Enter your own address and select Send sign-in link. Check your inbox and spam folder.
  2. Open the most recent link within 20 minutes. Enter the same address again and select Confirm sign-in.
  3. Wait for Signed in. Keep the 30-day option off on shared computers and sign out before leaving.
  4. Check for “Saved on the server” before you close. Keep a JSON backup if the server is unavailable.
!What is stored?

EFI BITCom stores your confirmed address and signed-in workshop work privately. The server copy includes task completion, reading position, answers and scratchpad. Sequence downloads and screenshots remain separate.

The confirmation link works once. Browser sign-in expires after 12 hours, or after 30 days if you choose to stay signed in. Signing out clears this browser’s signed-in copy and leaves the server copy available for your next sign-in. Contact the facilitator about access or removal.
