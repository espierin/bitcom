How to use this page

Start at the source

This is not a directory of every H&I tool. It prioritises primary publishers, maintained specifications and durable community resources. Each link is labelled by its role: EFI policy and education, reference nomenclature and sequence data, or external technical guidance.

For clinical or accreditation decisions, always consult the currently applicable EFI standards, local requirements and validated laboratory procedures. External specifications and software are informative unless explicitly adopted in those documents.

Maintenance commitment
BITCom reviews links and their status annually, and sooner after an EFI standards update or a material release of a referenced specification. Suggestions for a resource should name the owner, maintenance route and intended H&I use case.

Data standards and implementation

Standards that preserve meaning across systems

Genotype grammar

GL String and GLSC

Syntax for lossless text representation of HLA and KIR genotypes. GLSC binds the grammar to the relevant namespace and nomenclature version or creation date.

Open GLSC specification
Structured genotyping exchange

HML

Histoimmunogenetics Markup Language: a structured exchange format for HLA and KIR genotyping, with method, result and reference context.

Open HML repository
NGS reporting guidance

MIRING

Minimum Information for Reporting Immunogenomic NGS Genotyping defines the metadata needed to make HLA and KIR NGS results interpretable and reusable over time.

Curation rule

Prefer the source; label the status

Every resource record should identify its original publisher, owner, intended use, date checked and status: EFI standard, EFI information, external specification, reference database, educational material or research/community resource.

Do not mix approved EFI requirements with development work, internal files or vendor material. A link to a resource is not an endorsement of a particular assay, software product or clinical interpretation.

Suggest a resource
Send BITCom the stable URL, owner, version or date, intended audience and a short statement of its relevance to H&I. The committee can assess currency, authority, accessibility and maintenance before listing it.
