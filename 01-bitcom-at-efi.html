Bioinformatics & IT Committee

Supporting EFI with sound, practical and future-oriented digital and bioinformatics expertise.

Our purpose

Digital foundations for H&I

BITCom is EFI’s central advisory body for bioinformatics, digital infrastructure and information technology. It helps ensure that data, software and digital processes support quality, interoperability, education and scientific progress in Histocompatibility and Immunogenetics.

The committee’s formal mission is to promote secure, reliable and interoperable use of digital tools and data standards across EFI activities.

Meet the committee
Established in 2018
EFI’s published committee page confirms BITCom’s founding purpose: to support the Executive Committee and other EFI committees on IT-related matters.
