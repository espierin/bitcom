Activity overview

A concise public record of enduring work, rather than an internal project log.

2018

BITCom is established

EFI installs the Bioinformatics & IT Committee to support the Executive Committee and other committees on IT-related matters.

2020–2024

Digital development and data communication

EFI newsletters record work relating to websites, conference processes, educational technology and HLA data communication and storage.

2025

Laboratory bioinformatics teaching

EFI’s Prague teaching programme includes Bioinformatics and its use in the laboratory, with BITCom participation.

2026 onward

Formalised remit and regular review

The BITCom Procedure Manual defines the committee’s mission, structure, meeting rhythm and responsibilities for digital and bioinformatics advice.

Update rule

Publish only approved, dated activity

Each future update should state what happened, when it happened, who owns the item and where the canonical EFI source is. Omit member-specific tasks, meeting minutes and draft recommendations unless explicitly approved for public release.
