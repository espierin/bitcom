Activity overview

A committee with both memory and momentum.

This short timeline highlights work recorded in EFI communications. It gives future updates a clear context without turning the page into a technical project log.

2012–2018

From informal IT support to an EFI committee

Website-revision work began in 2012. The formally installed IT and Bioinformatics Committee followed at the 2018 EFI Assembly in Venice.

2019–2020

Digital foundations

EFI reported work on e-learning options, a move toward EasyChair, an integrated Federation & Conference app concept, and support for the corporate and conference websites.

2022–2024

Abstracts and data communication

BITCom supported a new abstract platform, collaboration with EFI Standards on HLA-data communication and storage, and a continued EasyChair commitment.

2025–2026

Standards, hands-on training and renewal

Activities include DaSH/HAML work, the Prague teaching session, hands-on bioinformatics training in Edinburgh, Summer School adaptation and two forthcoming committee vacancies.

What happens next
