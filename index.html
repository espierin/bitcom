The EFI Bioinformatics & IT Committee (BITCom) guides this workshop in two 45-minute blocks. Bring a laptop with a browser and keep a plain-text editor open for your sequences and notes. Follow the four steps using your assigned sequence and the reference you identify during the analysis. If this is your first time working with FASTA, start at Step 1 and keep the workshop open in one browser tab. Create a local folder named EFI-HLA-workshop for everything you save. Work on the case assigned by your facilitator. The Downloads page contains the reference and all case files. Sign in with email to save your workshop work on the server, or continue as a guest and download a JSON backup before closing.
