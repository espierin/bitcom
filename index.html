Start here

The EFI Bioinformatics & IT Committee (BITCom) guides this workshop in two 45-minute blocks. Bring a laptop with a browser and keep a plain-text editor open for your sequences and notes. Follow the four steps using your assigned sequence and the reference you identify during the analysis. If this is your first time working with FASTA, start at Step 1 and keep the workshop open in one browser tab. Create a local folder named EFI-HLA-workshop for everything you save. Complete the core case before trying the additional cases.
