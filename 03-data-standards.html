Data standards

Reliable data needs a common language.

BITCom works with EFI Standards and international collaborators to support clear communication, storage and exchange of immunogenetics data.

HLA typing

HML and structured results

EFI has reported that HML implementation in NGS software has advanced. BITCom’s focus is on recommendations that support meaningful, secure HLA data representation, communication and storage.

HLA antibodies

HAML through DaSH

BITCom supports the Data Standards Hackathon (DaSH) work around HLA Antibody Markup Language (HAML), first used for exchange of antibody assay results at IHIW18.

Interoperability

From data to clinical use

The HAML working group is developing the standard to meet diagnostic needs and to prepare for future HL7-FHIR integration.

Working across EFI and beyond

EFI’s 2024 report records BITCom’s work with the Standards Committee on data standards and a joint position paper with ASHI, NMDP and WMDA. The purpose is practical: promote data integrity, safety, FAIR principles and consistency in laboratories and research.
