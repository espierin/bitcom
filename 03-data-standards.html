Data standards

Common, well-governed data make laboratory, registry and research collaboration more reliable.

Role

From representation to responsible exchange

HLA genotyping data

BITCom supports implementation-oriented discussion of structured HLA genotyping information and its transfer between systems.

Antibody data

Work on HLA Antibody Markup Language (HAML) has been discussed through international data-standard initiatives, including DaSH.

Interoperability

The committee promotes data integrity, transparency, FAIR principles and practical interoperability, always in dialogue with EFI standards work.

Publication rule

Clear status is part of a useful standard

This page should link only to documents that are approved for public use. Every item needs an owner, version, publication date, review date and a clear label: EFI policy, external standard, educational resource or work in progress.

BITCom does not make a draft discussion normative by placing it on a public page.

Source trail
