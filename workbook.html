Your workshop record

Enter the case assigned by your facilitator and the reference allele you are using. Record file names so you can trace each result back to its input.

Step 1 · Genomic DNA to protein

Record what you obtained from the genomic sequence, annotation and translation. Include lengths and units. Keep the actual sequence files in your workshop folder as instructed.

Step 2 · Synthetic allele

Record your synthetic sequence analysis and why you chose a reference. Copy relevant result values and list the differences you observed. Note which numbering system you use.

Step 3 · Biological interpretation

Separate observations from interpretations. For each conclusion, say what evidence supports it and what remains uncertain. If a tool fails, record the fallback used.

Step 4 · Structure mapping

Record the structure and chain you selected, how your sequence positions map onto it, and what you see. Save images separately and record their file names here.
