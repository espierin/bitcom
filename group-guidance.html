Working together in a group of 40

For alignment, use the recommended online SMS2 route: each laptop calculates its own alignment. Follow its input-symbol and length checks first. For NetSurfP, practise one submission per participant or pair on a staggered schedule. Wait no longer than two minutes, then use the saved-results viewer. Keep job IDs and do not resubmit. If the service is down, use the screen examples to learn submission and proceed to archived data.

If a job is queued or fails, continue with the downloads below. Keep the job identifier and check the existing job later. Repeated submissions create extra work for the server.

  1. If neither alignment route is usable for step 2.7, open the complete core protein comparison. Each block gives its residue range. Vertical bars mark agreement and an asterisk marks the substitution. Record the changed position and both residues.
  2. For steps 3.3.1–3.3.2, select your exact input in the NetSurfP viewer, verify the protein and enter the residue position you identified. Interpret RSA yourself. Download the offline viewer and full data package before the workshop.

Facilitator guidance and service limits

EMBL-EBI asks for batches of at most 30 simultaneous Web Services jobs, waiting for completion before another batch. This is a fair-use limit, not reserved capacity. UniProt Align uses the EBI Clustal Omega service, so switching to that service directly does not provide independent capacity. NetSurfP documents sequence-size limits and a mirror, but gives no guarantee for 40 simultaneous jobs. SMS2 avoids the shared alignment queue; for NetSurfP, a two-minute waiting limit and archived results keep the lesson moving. Use downloaded sequences for the exact reference if GitHub or SFAT is slow. For a practice case, record the unavailable annotation and use only a verified matching CDS. ExPASy can cross-check the annotated reading frame when ORFfinder is unavailable.
