Facilitator guidance and service limits

EMBL-EBI asks for batches of at most 30 simultaneous Web Services jobs, waiting for completion before another batch. This is a fair-use limit, not reserved capacity. UniProt Align uses the EBI Clustal Omega service, so switching to that service directly does not provide independent capacity. NetSurfP documents sequence-size limits and a mirror, but gives no guarantee for 40 simultaneous jobs. SMS2 avoids the shared alignment queue; for NetSurfP, one shared demonstration plus saved results remains the classroom route. Use the downloaded reference sequences if GitHub or SFAT is slow, and ExPASy for translation if ORFfinder fails.
