Working together in a group of 40

Your facilitator will demonstrate one live alignment and one NetSurfP submission for the core pair. Everyone can then inspect the same saved results. Submit additional jobs only when your facilitator invites your group.

If a job is queued or fails, continue with the downloads below. Keep the job identifier and check the existing job later. Repeated submissions create extra work for the server.

  1. For step 2.7, open the complete core protein comparison. Each block gives its residue range. Vertical bars mark agreement and an asterisk marks the substitution. Record the changed position and both residues.
  2. For steps 3.3.1–3.3.2, download the saved NetSurfP predictions. Open the CSV in Excel or a text editor. Find precursor_position 212 for reference and variant and compare amino_acid and RSA.

The predictions come from a completed NetSurfP-3.0 run on the two supplied proteins, retrieved 5 November 2026. Reference raw result · Variant raw result.

Facilitator guidance and service limits

EMBL-EBI asks for batches of at most 30 simultaneous Web Services jobs, waiting for completion before another batch. This is a fair-use limit, not reserved capacity. UniProt Align uses the EBI Clustal Omega service, so switching to that service directly does not provide independent capacity. NetSurfP documents sequence-size limits and a mirror, but gives no guarantee for 40 simultaneous jobs. One shared demonstration plus saved results is the default classroom route. Use the downloaded reference sequences if GitHub or SFAT is slow, and ExPASy for translation if ORFfinder fails.
