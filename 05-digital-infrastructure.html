Digital EFI

Good infrastructure should feel uncomplicated to members.

BITCom helps EFI create dependable, future-oriented digital services for membership, learning and scientific exchange.

Web platforms

EFI’s digital home

Website development has been part of the committee’s remit since its formative years, with an emphasis on clear navigation, appropriate information security and a member-friendly experience.

Education

Learning environments

The committee assessed technical options for e-learning, including Moodle, and continues to support EFI’s educational platform.

Conferences

Abstract workflows

BITCom supports robust abstract submission, review and preparation for publication, together with scientific and local organising colleagues.

EasyChair: a sustained EFI service

EFI reported in 2024 a five-year commitment to continue using EasyChair for abstract management. Recent EFI2026 feedback described the revised platform as user-friendly and robust, with a more streamlined workflow.

Editorial principle: explain member-facing benefits, not technical implementation. Keep security, data protection and vendor arrangements in EFI’s appropriate governance documentation.
