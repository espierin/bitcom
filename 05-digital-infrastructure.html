Digital infrastructure

Member-facing technology should be dependable, understandable and designed to endure.

Advisory remit

Digital services that support EFI’s purpose

Website and information

BITCom advises on digital infrastructure and content systems so that important EFI information can be found, understood and maintained.

Education and collaboration

The committee assesses technical opportunities that enable practical learning and efficient collaboration without adding avoidable complexity.

Scientific workflows

It provides expertise relevant to digital processes supporting conferences, resources, data exchange and related EFI activities.

Public communication

Explain benefits; govern the details properly

Public pages should describe what a service allows an EFI member to do. Security architecture, supplier arrangements, contracts, permissions and operational incidents belong in the appropriate internal governance records.

Good publication practice
For each service, identify the business owner, technical owner, privacy contact, support route and planned review date.
