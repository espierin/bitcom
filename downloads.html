Case 01

Genomic DNA · CDS · Protein

!Detailed answer

Exon 4 substitution

Genomic position 1885 A→T maps to CDS position 635. The codon CAT changes to CTT, giving H212L in the precursor and H188L in mature numbering. The protein remains 365 residues long. The change is outside the ARD, in the α3 region. NetSurfP predicted RSA of about 0.331 for the reference histidine and 0.360 for the variant leucine. In PDB 3BO8, inspect chain A His 188.

Exon 3 splice-site exercise

The substitution at genomic position 1014 changes the acceptor AG to AT immediately before exon 3. This supports possible splice disruption. SFAT returned a full-length coding sequence in the workshop test, so the translation alone does not resolve the splice consequence.

Case 04

Genomic DNA · CDS · Protein

!Detailed answer

A*01:04:01:01N premature stop

The supplied A*01:04:01:01N example contains an early in-frame stop. Translation of the CDS reaches the first stop at codon 220, after 219 amino acids. The 660-base span includes that stop codon. Compare this with the 365-amino-acid reference. Check how the supplied protein represents the terminal stop.

Case 05

Genomic DNA · CDS · Protein

!Detailed answer

A*01:01:01:02N null allele

This case represents A*01:01:01:02N. Compare the genomic and coding inputs separately and record how they relate to the supplied short protein. The null designation concerns expression. Compare exons 2 and 3 to assess ARD identity, and consult the current IPD record for the documented expression and group assignments. A full-length supplied CDS alone cannot establish expression.
