Committee membership

Contribute to a practical, European conversation

The Procedure Manual asks BITCom to maintain a balanced mix of expertise across data standards, information security, interoperability and digital infrastructure. Members are appointed by the EFI Executive Committee following the committee’s nomination process.

Vacancies and desired competencies should be published by EFI only when confirmed. No open BITCom vacancy is claimed on this prototype.

How to apply
Use the official EFI Committee Member Application Form and follow the submission instructions on the EFI page. EFI Office is the appropriate contact for a current vacancy or eligibility question.

What BITCom needs

H&I insight

Experience with laboratory, registry, clinical or research settings in histocompatibility and immunogenetics.

Digital perspective

Bioinformatics, data management, information systems, software or interoperability expertise.

Collaborative approach

Readiness to contribute to working groups, reviews and communication across EFI committees.
