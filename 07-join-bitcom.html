Committee vacancies

Bring your perspective to BITCom.

Two positions will become available on the EFI Bioinformatics & IT Committee. We welcome EFI members who are interested in practical digital development, data or bioinformatics in H&I.

Application deadline: 31 January 2027.
Appointments take effect at the EFI Conference in Essen, Germany, in May 2027.

Who should apply?

You do not need to be senior

Curiosity counts

You do not need to be an experienced bioinformatician. We value colleagues with practical H&I experience and an interest in data, digital tools or education.

A European perspective

Bring your local experience

Help ensure that EFI developments make sense across different laboratories, countries and career stages.

A useful contribution

Work on real activities

Contribute to practical education, data standards and member-facing digital services, alongside a supportive international group.

How to apply

Simply complete the EFI Committee Member Application Form and send it to EFI Office. The form asks about your EFI membership, relevant skills and why you would like to contribute. Appointments are for three years and may be renewed, subject to EFI Executive Committee approval.

Download the application form

Submit to: efioffice@lumc.nl

Vacancies and desired competencies are reviewed against the committee’s skill mix. BITCom votes on nominations; appointments are then subject to final EFI Executive Committee approval. Questions or expressions of interest before applying are welcome via EFI Office.
