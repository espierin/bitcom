EFI2026 · Edinburgh

Bioinformatics hands-on sessions

At the 39th European Immunogenetics & Histocompatibility Conference in Edinburgh, BITCom delivered two practical bioinformatics sessions alongside the scientific programme. The sessions offered conference participants a focused opportunity to engage directly with methods, tools and data questions relevant to H&I laboratory and research practice.

These sessions formed part of BITCom’s commitment to make bioinformatics approachable, useful and connected to real-world work. They complement the conference’s dedicated bioinformatics programme and the associated Data Standards Hackathon (DaSH) meeting.
